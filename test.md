Proposed title
Development of a Smart Clear Aligner Monitoring System for Objective Assessment of Patient Wear-Time Compliance
Abstract
Background:
Patient compliance with prescribed clear-aligner wear time is an important determinant of treatment effectiveness. Conventional methods of monitoring compliance are largely dependent on patient self-reporting and retrospective assessment, which may not accurately reflect actual aligner wear. This study proposes the development of a smart clear-aligner monitoring system capable of objectively recording aligner wear and removal patterns.
Aim:
To develop and evaluate a miniature sensor-based system integrated with a reusable Smart Aligner Box for objective monitoring of clear-aligner wear time and real-time compliance reminders.
Methods:
A miniature, removable electronic tracker is proposed to be positioned within a dedicated housing incorporated into the clear aligner, avoiding direct embedding of electronic components during thermoforming. An infrared-based proximity/reflection sensing mechanism will be investigated to differentiate the worn and unworn states of the aligner. The tracker will communicate with a reusable Smart Aligner Box, which will receive, timestamp and store wear-time data for extended periods. The box will incorporate local data storage, a real-time clock, rechargeable power supply, wireless data transfer and an audible/visual reminder system. Configurable alerts will be investigated for prolonged aligner removal, while cumulative daily wear time will be compared with the prescribed 22-hour target. A dedicated mobile application will be developed to visualize daily, weekly and long-term compliance data.
Expected outcome:
The proposed system is expected to provide objective, time-stamped aligner wear data, automated re-wear reminders and longitudinal compliance monitoring while allowing the Smart Aligner Box to be reused between patients following secure data reassignment. The feasibility and accuracy of infrared-based wear-state detection will be evaluated under clinically relevant conditions.
Conclusion:
This proposed smart-alignment monitoring platform may provide a practical digital approach for objective assessment of clear-aligner compliance and could facilitate improved patient adherence and clinician monitoring during orthodontic treatment.
